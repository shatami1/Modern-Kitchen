# Image and font sources

Kitchen concepts were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-27. They are labeled as AI-generated inspiration and do not represent completed customer projects.

- kitchen-walnut: luxury modern Atlanta kitchen, walnut cabinets, travertine/stone island, brass lighting, leafy garden; 16:9 editorial architectural photograph.
- kitchen-before: edit of the walnut concept, preserving room and perspective while using dated honey oak cabinets, beige laminate, tiled floor and basic lights.
- kitchen-ivory: bright ivory Shaker kitchen, light oak island, brass details and opal pendants; editorial architectural photograph.

Original generation prompts are recorded in the task conversation. Optimized WebP versions are stored in assets/.
