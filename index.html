A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Let’s make
it clearer.

Where do you work?+

We serve Metro Atlanta, including Sandy Springs. Share your ZIP code when you request an estimate so we can confirm your location and project fit.

Can you coordinate my entire kitchen renovation?+

Yes. Accent can coordinate cabinetry, countertops, backsplash, lighting and electrical, plumbing, flooring and finishing. Your proposal will define the exact services and trades included in your project.

How much will my renovation cost?+

Cost depends on the size of the space, layout changes, selected cabinetry and materials, and the trade work needed. Share your ideas and an approximate budget, and we can start shaping a realistic scope.

What should I have ready for an estimate?+

A few photos, your ZIP code, approximate kitchen size, what you want to change and your preferred timing are a great start. You can attach photos when you send your estimate email.
